03 Supported devices and functions

The current version targets iPhone Duo running iOS 27.1 or later. Check device and system requirements before downloading. Capabilities also depend on hinge input actually provided by the system.

The app offers independent closed, partially open, and fully open records; daily and lifetime totals; history; personal daily limits; local JSON export; and widgets showing folds today and the remaining allowance.

The app currently has no accounts, developer-operated cloud synchronization, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising. Any download price is the price displayed by the App Store at that time.

04 Counting and coverage

  • Closed, partially open, and fully open movements are three separate metrics. Adding the last two together does not create a single consistent opening total.
  • Moving from closed to partially open counts once only after one continuous second. A shorter interval does not count. Fully opening after a confirmed partial opening adds a separate fully open event.
  • Returning from fully open to partially open adds no partially open event. Fully opening again can add another fully open event. Repeated state callbacks do not duplicate counts.
  • The first state in each continuous observation establishes a baseline. A recovered state cannot reconstruct movements missed during an interruption.
  • Records reflect valid events actually received and saved. The app also attempts to record background callbacks. Suspension, Lock Screen use, app exit, or missing input may leave gaps. Complete day-long coverage is not guaranteed.
  • Widgets display saved statistics. The system schedules refreshes; widgets do not collect movements independently or guarantee an immediate update after each movement.

System scheduling, input availability, storage space, system time, and the statistics time zone may affect recording and display. Consider the app’s pause and synchronization messages when interpreting results.

05 Goals and limits

You set personal daily goals for each metric independently. They are off by default. An initial goal may apply today; later changes normally default to tomorrow, and disabling takes effect immediately. Check the effective date shown before saving.

Recording continues at and beyond a personal limit. A goal is a reminder: it does not restrict the hinge, block other apps, or measure time spent in other apps.

Movement counts cannot determine creases, screen health, hardware life, or warranty status. The app does not guarantee fewer creases, longer device life, or a particular focus outcome. Use the device normally and follow its manufacturer’s care guidance.

06 Local data and export

Settings lets you view, export, or clear records. Clearing resets counts, history, and goals within the scope shown in the confirmation and cannot be undone in the app.

You initiate exports and choose their destination and recipients. Keep and manage copies carefully. The current version does not import JSON for restoration, and exported files are not guaranteed to restore app data.

Clearing, system data management, device failure, or other events may cause data loss. Backups, device transfers, and exported copies depend on the relevant system or service. We have no remote archive from which to restore your local data.

For data categories, support emails, website access, and rights requests, read the Privacy Policy.

07 Safety and minors

Obtain the app through trusted official distribution channels, protect device access, and avoid exposing unrelated personal records when exporting or seeking help. Do not treat statistics as professional hardware testing, medical, legal, or other expert advice.

Minors should use the app with guardian guidance and obtain consent where required by law. Do not include unrelated information about minors in support emails.
